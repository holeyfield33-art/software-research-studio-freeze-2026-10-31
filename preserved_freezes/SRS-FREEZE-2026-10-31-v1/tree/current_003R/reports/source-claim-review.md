## Standards and normativity

- IEEE’s official catalog listing was checked for ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026: active status, publication/approval metadata, and supersession of 2017 are stated in the source record. The full normative text was not inspected, and organizational adoption is unknown; therefore no automatic conformity or mandatory gate claim is permitted.
- NIST SP 800-218 (SSDF 1.1) is used as **recommended, adaptable, informative guidance**. Its source claim is explicitly a synthesis; it does not establish universal gates, ordering, or completion.
- NIST SP 800-53 is not referenced by the three fixture selections or source-claim review set, so no edition/normativity claim about it is made here.

## Evidence limits

Pinned repository files and tree inventories establish file/path presence or authored test/workflow content at a commit, not execution or compliance. The fixtures retain `not_run` status for tests, CI, and releases. Source locators are exact source-record claim locators; source limitations and evidence classes remain visible.
